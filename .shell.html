Super Synth Lab - Instrument

Version: Super Synth Lab - Instrument v1.10.1163

Super Synth Lab - Instrument is a focused synthesizer with 17+ synth engines, effects chain, and MIDI support. Play, shape your sound, and perform -- all from 5 streamlined screens.

What can Super Synth Lab - Instrument do?
  • Play with piano keyboard, isomorphic grids, pads, guitar fretboard, and more
  • Choose from 17+ synthesis engines: Subtractive, FM, Physical Modelling, Granular, Additive, and more
  • Shape your sound with ADSR envelopes and filters
  • Apply 21+ effects: reverb, delay, chorus, distortion, and more
  • Connect MIDI keyboards and controllers
Privacy

Super Synth Lab - Instrument runs entirely in your browser. No data is collected, no audio is recorded or transmitted, no accounts required. All settings are stored locally on your device.
